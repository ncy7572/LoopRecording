About Loop Recording

Loop Recording keeps a rolling microphone buffer while the app is open, so you can jump back, replay recent audio, and export recordings as WAV files when you choose.

  • Adjustable rolling buffer duration
  • Live waveform display
  • Playback and 15-second skip controls
  • WAV export through the iOS share sheet
  • Input device selection when supported by iOS

Privacy Policy

Loop Recording uses microphone access to record audio into a rolling buffer on your device while the app is open.

Audio is processed locally on your device. Loop Recording does not upload audio to our servers, does not create user accounts, does not collect personal information, does not use third-party analytics, and does not track users.

When you export a recording, the file is shared only through the iOS sharing options that you select.
